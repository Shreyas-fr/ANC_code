## Why FullSubNet is better offline: evidence and limits

Observed: FullSubNet improves paired means over Step-500 for all four metrics on the fixed 590 clips, with 95% paired bootstrap intervals above zero. Grouped by the available `origin` labels, it also improves all four metrics for both `EXISTING_PROXY` (n=148) and `IoBT_GUNFIRE` (n=442); details are appended to [the FullSubNet baseline](../docs/FULLSUBNET_BASELINE.md).

Plausible mechanism, not an isolated causal result: its full-band recurrent path can use broad spectral context while its sub-band path models local frequency structure; these complementary cues may better separate noise from speech in the evaluated domain. Training-domain fit, the different sample rate/model objective, and checkpoint differences also matter. The benchmark does not isolate those factors, and there are no event-level transient labels or blinded speech-deletion annotations. We therefore cannot claim the architecture alone caused the gain.

## Low-SNR explanation

At -10 dB input, the Step-500 baseline has 0/30 joint passes; FullSubNet also has 0/30. At -5 dB, Step-500 has 3/63 and FullSubNet 2/63. Under single-channel observation, overlapping speech/noise components are not uniquely separable; suppressing noise can also attenuate low-energy phonetic cues, and impulsive interference can remain perceptually intrusive. The measured SNR gain alongside weak STOI/PESQ is consistent with that mechanism, but does not prove it. Low-SNR rows are heavily concentrated in the gunfire-origin subset, so SNR and source domain are confounded. Better causal evidence needs event/speech-preservation annotation and controlled comparisons, not an architectural guess.

## Stage 4 decision

Best next candidate is **GTCRN inference-only E1**, because a first-party lightweight implementation and pretrained checkpoints are publicly described, and that evaluation can test both quality and edge-efficiency without new training. The isolated environment, one-file smoke, and 590-clip evaluation are complete; no further experiment is authorized without user review. Do not upload the private corpus. Step-500 stays protected; FullSubNet remains the strongest completed offline comparison, not a deployment replacement.
